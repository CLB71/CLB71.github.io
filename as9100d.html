AS9100D Readiness Check

24 questions across the quality management system, risk, operational planning, configuration, supplier & production control, monitoring, and audit/review. Answer as many questions as apply — skip any that don't — to receive an instant preliminary readiness report. Takes about 5 minutes, no email required.

SCALE: 1 = Not in place  ·  2 = Informal / ad hoc  ·  3 = Partially defined  ·  4 = Defined and followed  ·  5 = Mature, continuously improved
01Quality Management System
How mature is your documented quality management system?
How well do quality objectives cascade into day-to-day operations?
How effectively does leadership demonstrate commitment to the QMS?
02Risk Management
How systematically are risks identified and assessed across operations?
How well are risk mitigation actions tracked through to closure?
How integrated is risk-based thinking into day-to-day decision-making?
03Operational Planning & Product Realization
How disciplined is your contract/order review process before production starts?
How well are product realization requirements planned and controlled?
How effectively are special requirements (customer, statutory, regulatory) identified and flowed down?
04Configuration Management
How rigorously is product and process configuration identified and controlled?
How well are engineering changes reflected across all affected documentation?
How reliably can you reconstruct a product's configuration history?
05Supplier Control
How rigorous is your supplier selection and approval process?
How well is supplier performance monitored and acted on?
How effectively is flow-down of requirements to suppliers verified?
06Production Control
How well controlled is production (work instructions, controlled conditions)?
How reliable is your process for verifying product at appropriate stages?
How well controlled are special processes requiring qualification?
07Monitoring, Measurement & Nonconformity
How effective is your process for monitoring and measuring product/process conformance?
How well are nonconforming products identified, segregated and controlled?
How robust is your corrective action process at addressing root cause?
08Internal Audit & Management Review
How structured and risk-based is your internal audit program?
How effectively does management review drive improvement decisions?
How well are audit and review outputs tracked through to closure?
