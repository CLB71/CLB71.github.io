Manufacturing Readiness Assessment

24 questions across engineering, industrialization, production, quality and organization. Answer honestly — this takes about 5 minutes and gives you an instant, downloadable readiness report, no email required.

SCALE: 1 = Not in place  ·  2 = Informal / ad hoc  ·  3 = Partially defined  ·  4 = Defined and followed  ·  5 = Mature, continuously improved
01Engineering
How mature is your product design and engineering process?
How well controlled are engineering drawings and documents?
How disciplined is your engineering change (ECN) process?
How well is product configuration tracked and controlled?
How smooth is the handover from engineering to manufacturing?
02Industrialization
How well defined are your manufacturing processes?
How complete and current are shop-floor work instructions?
How rigorously are new processes validated before production?
How structured is your production readiness review process?
How well managed is tooling and equipment qualification/maintenance?
03Production
How reliable is your production planning and scheduling?
How well do you understand and manage production capacity?
How consistently are production KPIs tracked and acted on?
How effectively are bottlenecks identified and resolved?
How robust is your traceability and non-conformance handling?
04Quality
How mature is your quality management system?
How effective is your internal audit program?
How robust is your corrective / preventive action process?
How well do you manage supplier quality?
How ready is your organization for AS9100D requirements?
05Organization
How clearly defined are roles and ownership?
How effective is communication across departments?
How structured is leadership's review of performance?
How well does the organization measure and use performance data?
