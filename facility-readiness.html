Aerospace Manufacturing Facility Readiness Planner

A structured readiness assessment for establishing or industrializing an aerospace manufacturing facility — engineering, manufacturing engineering, production, quality, supply chain, equipment, workforce and certification readiness.

Answer the questions that apply to your facility — skip any that don't. Your results identify strengths, potential gaps and areas requiring further attention as you move toward production. 24 questions, about 5 minutes, no email required.

How prepared is the facility for its target certification?

How qualified and secured are the suppliers required for this facility?
