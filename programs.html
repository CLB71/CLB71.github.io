04 — Selected Programs

A sample of the aircraft and UAV programs behind the numbers.

Urban Mobility — UAE

Project DROXY

Led the mechanical architecture and structural development of an electric autonomous VTOL drone, integrating high-torque propulsion with autonomous navigation sensors. Presented at GITEX Technology Week, Dubai.

Heavy-Lift UAV — 250kg Payload

Autonomous Heavy-Lift UAV (F150)

Airframe redesign in 7075-T6 aluminum and composites for a GPS-denied autonomous platform. Result: +40% structural strength, +25% payload capacity, 99.2% navigation accuracy.

Mass-Production Aircraft

ULM Storm 300

Design lead for structural evolution and mass-production readiness of an ultralight aircraft — 15% weight reduction, improved safety margins, and a 30% increase in manufacturing output.

Composite / Metallic Integration

ULM Rally 105 UL

Managed the engineering interface between a composite fuselage and aluminum-alloy wings, achieving a 15% weight reduction while meeting LTF-UL and EASA-aligned airworthiness standards.
