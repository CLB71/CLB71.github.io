05 — Engagement Models

How the work actually gets structured.

Depending on what you need — consulting engagement, embedded support, or a full-time role.

Advisory

Independent Assessment

Independent assessment and technical or operational strategy — the outside read a program needs before committing resources.

Best suited when: you need an independent expert opinion — readiness assessments, technical reviews, industrialization strategy, or due diligence.

Implementation

Hands-On Delivery

Hands-on development and deployment of the processes and systems themselves, working alongside your existing team.

Best suited when: you need manufacturing system implementation, process development, production readiness, or AS9100D implementation support.

Embedded Support

Direct Team Support

Direct support to Engineering, Production, Manufacturing or Quality leadership — for the periods where a program needs an experienced hand inside the organization, not just advice from outside it.

Best suited when: a program needs interim manufacturing or industrialization leadership, production management, quality leadership, or program recovery support.
