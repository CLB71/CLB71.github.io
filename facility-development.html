Services

Hands-on support building or industrializing an aerospace manufacturing facility.

Standing up a new facility, or industrializing an existing site for aerospace work, means getting engineering, equipment, workforce, quality and supply chain right together — not in sequence, and not as an afterthought once the building exists. This is direct, hands-on engagement — working the plan alongside the team, not handing over a report and stepping back.

The free Facility Readiness Planner is a good starting point for a self-assessment of where a facility stands today. This page describes what it looks like to work through the gaps it surfaces — the actual industrialization engagement.

What's Covered

Scoped to the facility and phase — typically drawing from the following.

Site & Layout Planning Equipment Selection & Procurement Support Process Architecture Workforce Planning & Hiring Support Quality System Standup (AS9100D) Supply Chain Development Certification Readiness Production Ramp-Up
How It Works

The same Assess, Design, Implement, Transfer sequence behind every engagement — applied to a facility.

A.

Assess

Where the facility stands today against what the program actually requires — site, equipment, workforce, quality system and supply chain, not just floor space.

B.

Design

A facility and process architecture built around the products it needs to produce — layout, equipment specification, staffing plan and quality system designed together.

C.

Implement

Working the plan directly — equipment bring-up, process qualification, workforce training, quality system standup and certification-body engagement.

D.

Transfer

Production ramp-up with the operation left in the hands of a team that owns it — not dependent on outside support to keep running.

Client identities, site details and program-specific information are held in confidence — public examples are presented at a non-confidential level.
